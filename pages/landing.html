CEL — Core Emergency Language

Humans and machines share the same words. You can say disaster.fire.wildland out loud; a schema can validate it. A site can sit in response.isolate_load without anyone translating EN0205 or a CAP XML blob first.

Stewarded by Tyler Eno. Anyone may implement it. CEL is not sold as a product.

The contract

  • Published schemas — every $id, dereferenceable
  • manifest.json — machine-readable identifier index
  • Canonical identifier base: https://tylereno.me/cel/schemas/

JSON Schema Draft 2020-12. Pin a commit or tag rather than fetching by URL if you need reproducibility.

The language

  • taxonomies/disasters.yaml — the incident words
  • response_machines/ — response words and allowed transitions
  • crosswalks/ — USGS / GDACS / NWS CAP → CEL
  • feeds/matrix.yaml — the ingest source matrix
  • normative/ — RFCs (served as plain text)

What this is not

  • Not a runtime, ingest service, or dashboard — CEL is the vocabulary and its schemas.
  • Not a copy of the UNDRR HIP tree, and not a fork of CAP. HIP codes appear as profiles on CEL leaves, not as the identifiers crews use.
  • Not commissioning evidence — that question belongs to OIDF. CEL is its sibling, not part of it.

v0 is a lab release: five schemas, one incident taxonomy, five response states.
