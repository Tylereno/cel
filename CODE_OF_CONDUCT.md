# Contributor Code of Conduct

We want CEL discussions to be technical, welcoming, and useful to people
working across emergency management, infrastructure, data, and open source.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and clear technical reasoning.
- Welcome different experience levels and implementation backgrounds.
- Keep proposals focused on the documented project boundary.
- Protect private, sensitive, and operational information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private or identifying information without consent.
- Deliberately introducing secrets, customer data, or unsafe operational claims.
- Misrepresenting a language/specification as an alert, policy, certification, or
  actuator.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation
when behavior violates this policy. Report concerns privately to the project
maintainers through GitHub rather than posting sensitive details publicly.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant. It applies to project
issues, discussions, pull requests, and other project-managed spaces.
